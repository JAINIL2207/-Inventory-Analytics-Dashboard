📊 ShopVista — Sales & Inventory Analytics Dashboard

📊 Project Title

ShopVista Sales & Inventory Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

🎯 Short Description / Purpose

ShopVista Sales & Inventory Analytics Dashboard is a single Power BI report designed to provide an interactive view of sales performance, profitability, customer activity, inventory availability, warehouse stock, supplier contribution, and category performance.

The dashboard combines executive-level KPIs with detailed inventory analysis so that business users can quickly understand current performance and identify areas that require operational attention.

🎯 Project Purpose

The main purpose of this dashboard is to:

Monitor overall sales and profit performance.

Compare actual sales with the sales target.

Track orders, quantity sold, and customer metrics.

Monitor available, allocated, and remaining inventory.

Analyze warehouse stock distribution.

Understand supplier contribution to sales.

Identify category-level profit performance.

Monitor payment, damaged-order, and operational metrics.

Support data-driven sales and inventory decisions.

💡 Business Insights

💰 Sales Performance

Total sales are approximately 2.23M, significantly above the 500K sales target.

The dashboard shows approximately 345.52% performance above the target, indicating strong sales achievement.

An AOV of 2.26K indicates the average revenue generated per order.

With 986 total orders, the dashboard provides a clear view of order-driven revenue performance.

📈 Profitability

Total profit is approximately 443.43K.

Profit per order is approximately 449.73.

Category-level profit analysis helps identify which categories are contributing most strongly to profitability.

The category chart indicates Kitchen as the strongest visible profit contributor, followed by categories such as Fashion and Sports.

📦 Inventory Position

The business currently has approximately 103K available units.

Around 30K units are allocated.

Approximately 73K units remain available after allocation.

This indicates that a substantial portion of inventory remains available for future allocation or sales activity.

🏭 Warehouse Insights

The business operates across 10 warehouses.

Warehouse stock varies across locations, allowing management to identify warehouses with relatively higher or lower stock levels.

Warehouse-level comparison can support inventory balancing and stock redistribution decisions.

🤝 Supplier Insights

The dashboard tracks 183 suppliers.

The Supplier Contribution by Sales treemap shows that supplier contribution is not evenly distributed.

Major suppliers such as Global Supplies, Nova Imports, TechSource, Prime Traders, Urban Works, Apex Distribution and SmartHub represent visible portions of sales contribution.

This analysis can support supplier performance evaluation and sourcing decisions.

🏷️ Category Insights

Category-level analysis provides visibility into remaining stock and profit contribution.

The Available Stock After Allocation visual helps compare inventory distribution across categories.

The Category Profit Distribution visual helps management focus on categories generating stronger profitability.

👥 Customer & Order Insights

204 B2B customers are tracked in the dashboard.

320 debit card users are included in the customer/payment analysis.

Average quantity per order is approximately 3.02 units.

These metrics can help understand customer purchasing behavior and order composition.

💳 Payment Insights

Paid amount is approximately 699.75K.

Pending amount is approximately 661.97K.

The relatively large pending amount makes payment-status monitoring an important operational consideration.

⚠️ Operational Quality

The dashboard records 15 damaged orders.

Tracking damaged orders can help management monitor operational quality and identify areas for process improvement.
